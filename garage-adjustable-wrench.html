Acquired 1974

Adjustable Crescent Wrench

The Garage Room

Heavy, cold to the touch, and reassuringly solid in the palm. Its knurled worm gear glides with a faint metallic scrape, narrowing or widening its jaws to fit whatever bolt the afternoon demands. Stamped with patent numbers long expired, its chrome plating has chipped away at the edges to reveal dull, honest carbon steel. It carries no pretense of specialized genius—only the steady promise that with a slight roll of the thumb, it will adapt to whatever is broken.

Fun Fact
The adjustable wrench design was originally patented in 1891 by Swedish inventor Johan Petter Johansson, who created it so he wouldn't have to lug an entire toolbox of fixed spanners on his rounds.
