Granite Mortar and Pestle

Kitchen Collection • Catalog No. K-05

Granite Mortar and Pestle. Acquired 1971. Hewn from speckled granite, it crushes spices through quiet friction and enduring gravity. Its textured bowl releases fragrant oils with patient strikes.

Curator's Note • Fun Fact

Stone mortars predate the invention of agriculture.
