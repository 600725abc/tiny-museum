Classic Moka Pot

Kitchen Collection • Catalog No. K-01

Cast in matte, seasoned aluminum and sculpted into eight distinct facets, the Moka pot is less an appliance than an intimate morning ritual. It relies on no electrical coils or digital timers—only elementary thermodynamics, thermal expansion, and a patient flame. Over decades of stovetop devotion, its lower water chamber has acquired mineral rings, while the central chimney continues to sing its rhythmic, bubbling hiss that signals morning has arrived.

Curator's Note • Fun Fact

The original 1933 design by Alfonso Bialetti was inspired by early washing machines of the era, which boiled soapy water inside a central pipe that bubbled upwards over clothes.
