Brass Paperclip

Desk Collection • Room 02

Brass Paperclip. Acquired 1961. Formed from a single ribbon of spring brass, it binds transient pages without piercing fiber. Its quiet torsion respects deliberate thought.

Curator's Note • Fun Fact

The double-loop design was patented in 1899 as the Gem clip.
