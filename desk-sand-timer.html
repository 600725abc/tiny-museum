Three-Minute Sand Timer

Desk Collection • Room 02

Enclosed in hand-turned walnut and brass pillars, this blown-glass ampoule holds three minutes of quartz sand. Ticking silently without alarms or electronics, its organic trickle visualizes passing moments, measuring reflection one falling grain at a time.

Curator's Note • Fun Fact

Traditional maritime sandglasses often utilized finely crushed eggshells or marble dust rather than beach sand, which tended to absorb humidity at sea and clog the narrow glass orifice.
