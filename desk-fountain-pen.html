Brass Fountain Pen

Desk Collection • Room 02

Cast in solid brass with an iridium-tipped nib, this fountain pen has weathered decades of ink-stained deliberation. In hand, it carries a grounding gravity, demanding thoughtful cadence while transforming fleeting ideas into enduring human record.

Curator's Note • Fun Fact

Early fountain pen inks were often iron gall formulations made from crushed oak galls; over centuries, their mild acidity literally etched historical manuscripts directly into the paper fibers.
