Acquired 1981

Steel Tape Measure

The Garage Room

Cased in scarred chrome-finished plastic and weighted with coiled spring steel, this compact cylinder holds twenty-five feet of tangible truth. When pulled, it gives off a signature abrasive hiss, bending gently under gravity until it snaps back with startling, sharp vitality. Its hooked tang wiggles intentionally by fractions of a millimeter—a built-in concession to true zero, accommodating both push and pull measurements with humble precision.

Fun Fact
The loose metal rivet hook at the tip isn't broken or loose from wear—the play exactly equals the thickness of the metal hook itself (usually 1/16th of an inch), ensuring accurate inside and outside measurements.
