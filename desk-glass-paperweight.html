Acquired: 1982

Glass Paperweight

Desk Collection • Room 02

Forged from solid soda-lime glass, this hemispherical paperweight anchors manuscripts against wandering breezes. Suspended micro-bubbles catch the afternoon light, offering a luminous reminder that quiet, steady weight can keep our scattering ideas grounded.

Curator's Note • Fun Fact

During the mid-19th century, decorative glass paperweights became celebrated status symbols on Victorian writing desks, frequently bought as luxury souvenirs at universal world expositions.
