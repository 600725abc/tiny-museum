Vintage Metal & Wood Stapler

Desk Collection • Room 02

Uniting polished red oak with nickel-plated steel, this vintage stapler was built to outlast its owners. A firm press delivers a decisive mechanical snap, clinching paper and sealing agreements with tactile, enduring finality.

Curator's Note • Fun Fact

King Louis XV of France is widely credited with commissioning the earliest known stapler in the 18th century, with every miniature fastener inscribed with his royal court crest.
